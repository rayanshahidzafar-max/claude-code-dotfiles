# Emotion

## Which feelings make people share

- People share what moves them. Sharing a feeling also binds the sharer to the friend who feels it too. [Berger ch3]
- A dry science piece made the most-shared list because it filled readers with awe, and awe-inspiring articles were markedly more likely to be shared. [Berger ch3]
- Sad articles were less likely to be shared, and positive ones more, yet anger and anxiety raised sharing too. The deciding factor is arousal, not pleasantness. [Berger ch3]
- High-arousal feelings (awe, excitement, amusement, anger, anxiety, disgust) fire people up to act. Low-arousal feelings (sadness, contentment) quiet them down. [Berger ch3]
- Funny things spread because amusement is arousing, not just because they're funny. [Berger ch3]
- Raising the anger or the humour in the same story raised how often people passed it on. [Berger ch3]
- Even physical arousal from running on the spot made people more likely to forward an unrelated article. Put messages where people are already fired up. [Berger ch3]
- Ask whether a piece will make the viewer laugh, cry, smile, get angry or feel strongly. If not, it won't be shared. [Kane ch3]

## Focusing on feelings

- Information alone rarely changes behaviour; people mostly know the facts already. Lead with what they feel. [Berger ch3]
- Even a search engine can move people, as a short love story told only through search queries did. [Berger ch3]
- Ask why the thing matters, then why that matters, a few times over, until you reach the feeling underneath. [Berger ch3]
- Want people fired up about a cause? Make them angry at the problem or inspired that they can change it, not sad about it. [Berger ch3]
- Disgust can carry a health message as long as it wakes people up rather than drags them down. [Berger ch3]
- Feeling makes the purchase and reasons defend it afterwards, so give them the feeling first and the facts to defend it afterwards. [Sugarman ch11]
- Relatable truth from the creator's own life, told with some vulnerability, connects better than polish. [Kane ch5]
- Lighten the mood of the viewer for a few seconds and you've done your job well. [Kane ch5]

## When the fire turns on you

- Anger spreads complaints as fast as praise. A musician's song about a broken guitar cost an airline far more than fixing the guitar would have. [Berger ch3]
- Watch for angry words in what people post and deal with the problem before it becomes a movement. [Berger ch3]
- High production values don't make a piece shareable. It has to connect emotionally to cross from good to passed on. [Kane ch5]

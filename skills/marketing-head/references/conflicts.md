# Conflict Rulings

The head decides. Each row names the clash, the ruling, and why.

| # | Clash | Ruling | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jab/hook ratio: 3:1 vs "no fixed blueprint" vs 4:1 | Start at 3 jabs : 1 right hook; let engagement set each platform's ratio after two weeks | a default to start, data to tune |
| 2 | Who writes the hook | `viral-hooks` writes the opener; `audience-growth` makes the test variations; `slippery-copy` owns everything after line one | one owner per part |
| 3 | Bait openings | Banned everywhere; the hook must match the content | clickbait wins the click and loses the follower |
| 4 | Virality vs retention first | Build the return loop for the core audience, then push spread | a spike on a product nobody returns to leaks |
| 5 | Paid vs organic | Small paid tests to find winners; boost only organic winners; never boost weak creative | Kane, Vaynerchuk and Berger agree on this middle |
| 6 | Asking for shares | Never a bare "share this" or "tag a friend"; give a reason for one friend; rewards pay in core value | asking bluntly and paying cash both kill the urge |
| 7 | Long vs short copy | Social posts short; the page behind the click as long as price and complexity need | each medium has its own length |
| 8 | Selling in posts | Jabs don't sell; the right hook sells simply, one CTA, built for mobile | the jab earns the hook |
| 9 | Repurposing | Cut and re-edit freely, but rebuild format, length and opening for each platform | native beats pasted |
| 10 | Trendjacking vs lasting triggers | Trendjack for reach; tie the brand to a recurring everyday cue for recall | trends fade, cues repeat |
| 11 | Negative emotion | Anger at a shared problem only; never at people or customers; never invented outrage | high arousal spreads, but trust is the asset |
| 12 | Fear and hope claims | Name a real risk once, then the cure; never imply results you can't back | honesty rules every persuasion skill |
| 13 | Metrics | Shares, saves and downstream actions beat views and likes; community judged as brand marketing over quarters | vanity metrics mislead |
| 14 | Audience size vs closeness | Grow the audience wide and the community narrow and deep | bigger dilutes connection |
| 15 | Own platform vs theirs | Gather where people already are; keep an owned line (newsletter) beside it | rented reach can vanish |
| 16 | Gamification | Reward status and progress toward the member's own goal; no points for their own sake, no cash speculation | rewards must fit the core value |
| 17 | Cliffhangers / "part two" | Each piece pays off its own promise first; the open loop is extra | an unpaid promise breaks trust |
| 18 | Notifications and reminders | Ask permission after the first reward; only trigger toward real value; never nag | owned triggers work until they annoy |
| 19 | Data vs gut | Data decides; instinct writes hypotheses; disagreements get one more cheap test | both have failed alone |
| 20 | Offer naming | `100m-offers` names the offer package; `name-craft` names the brand or product; `viral-hooks` writes the tagline | one owner per name |
| 21 | Brand story ownership | `storybrand` sets the one-liner; `expert-secrets-playbook` sets the big idea; `brand-community` sets the purpose; all must agree before content goes out | one story, many outfits |
| 22 | Em dashes and voice | `anti-ai-writing` has the final say on voice in all marketing copy | it runs last |
| 23 | Paid tools | Credit- or money-spending tools run only after the user agrees to the cost | the user's money, the user's call |
| 24 | Any other tie | The skill closest to the deliverable wins | the deliverable is the point |

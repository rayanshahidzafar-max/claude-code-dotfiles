# What Each Skill Does in the Room

## The new marketing skills

| Skill | Its job in the room | Hands back |
|---|---|---|
| `contagious-content` | STEPPS check on every piece; finds the remarkable thing, the trigger, the emotion, the visible signal, the useful part and the story | contagion sheet |
| `habit-hooks` | the return loop: internal trigger, easy action, variable reward, investment; open loops and "part two" for series | hook map |
| `audience-growth` | hook point, 5–10+ variations per concept, targeting, small paid tests, alliances, per-platform growth | variation sheet |
| `slippery-copy` | captions, ads, emails and pages that slide to the action; triggers, objections, CTA, edit pass | copy sheet |
| `platform-native` | jab plan, native format per platform, the right hook, trendjacking, community management | jab plan |
| `brand-community` | follower → audience → community; purpose, gathering, rituals, polls, replies, ladder, measurement | community plan |
| `growth-experiments` | must-have check, aha moment, North Star, ICE backlog, weekly tests, loops, activation, retention, pricing | growth board |

## Hand-offs

- `contagious-content` → `audience-growth`: the contagion sheet's piece goes to variation testing.
- `audience-growth` → `platform-native`: the winning variation gets its native form per platform.
- `platform-native` → `slippery-copy`: the right hook's caption and landing copy.
- `habit-hooks` ↔ `brand-community`: the community supplies the tribe reward and stored value; the hook map shows why members return.
- `growth-experiments` takes every other skill's idea into the ICE backlog and decides what gets tested first.

# Modes: each skill's exact job

## The series folder (shared by every mode)

```
stories/series/<series-slug>/
  series-outline.md   series-engine   the pitch: premise line, engines, units, the first six books
  series-bible.md     series-engine   the living canon; every fact tagged with the book that set it
  loop-ledger.md      sequel-craft    every plant, clue and open question, with its payoff book
  reader-path.md      series-doctor   reading order, entry points, prequels, spin-offs, hooks
stories/NNN-slug/     story-forge     one folder per installment, with a SERIES block in 00-brief.md
```

When earlier installments live in this layout, read each one's `08-final.md` for the text, and its `02-design.md` and revision log for the decisions behind it. The controlling idea is noted at the bottom of each revision log; the new book must honor what earlier books set up.

## SEQUEL

1. **`series-doctor`** runs the checkup on everything written so far: type, pattern, links, last pages, what's stuck. It asks what readers loved, from reviews, beta notes, or the user's own favorite scenes.
2. **`series-engine`** confirms the engine still runs, places this book in its unit or season, and moves each long arc to this book's position. It opens or updates the bible.
3. **`sequel-craft`** writes the installment plan: the question answered and the question asked, the twist on what readers loved, stakes raised or changed, the pattern broken, payoffs due, new plants, the opening and recap method, and the ending. It writes the ledger rows.
4. **The STORY lane** (SKILL.md, Step 3).
5. **The launch pack.**

## PREQUEL

1. **`series-engine`** pulls the canon timeline: everything the prequel may not contradict, who can know what, and where the prequel sits in story order and in publication order.
2. **`sequel-craft`** runs its prequel method: the moment (a big event the main books mention but never tell), whose story it is, and how dramatic irony replaces mystery. It writes the installment plan and the ledger rows the prequel pays off or opens.
3. **`series-doctor`** places it on the reader path (book zero, a reader gift, a companion, a prequel series) and decides how book one's back matter points to it.
4. **The STORY lane.**
5. **The launch pack.** The reader-gift step usually applies.

## CONTINUATION

1. **`series-doctor`** decides between extending onto a new arc, relaunching with a new premise, a follow-up years later, and ending. For a standalone becoming a series, it first checks whether the story gives the lead a usual task, an engine, at all.
2. **`series-engine`** designs the new arc or season (or the whole series, if it was a standalone), including its exits.
3. **`sequel-craft`** writes the installment plan for the first book of the continuation.
4. **The STORY lane.**
5. **The launch pack.**

## SPIN-OFF (side stories, crossovers, companions and second series too)

1. **`series-doctor`** settles what carries over (the world, a character, the tone), the timing, and how the old series hands readers on.
2. **`series-engine`** builds the spin-off's own pilot, with every set-up element in its own first book, inside the shared bible. It labels the work with its vocabulary: sequel, prequel, sidequel, spin-off, crossover.
3. **`sequel-craft`** sets the links (cameos, spoiler-free references to the original, loops that cross between series), then writes the installment plan.
4. **The STORY lane.**
5. **The launch pack.**

## SERIES PLAN

1. **`series-engine`** writes the series outline: premise line, engines, type, series question, units or seasons, arcs, cast and world for series potential, pilot type, and the bible.
2. **`series-doctor`** checks the type and pattern, names their built-in weaknesses and the exits, and drafts the reader path.
3. **`sequel-craft`** builds the blueprint (each book's question asked and answered) and the first ledger rows, the plants book one must carry.
4. **The STORY lane for book one,** only if the user wants it written now.
5. **The launch pack:** the series line and series-page copy only, unless more is asked for.

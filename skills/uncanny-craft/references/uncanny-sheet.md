# The Uncanny Sheet

Fill this in before drafting, then paste it into the story folder's `02-design.md` under an `UNCANNY SHEET` heading. `story-design` reads it before the step-outline, and `reader-brain` checks it at its station.

```
UNCANNY SHEET — <working title>

THE FAMILIAR THING:  <the home / face / place / routine / self that turns>
HOW IT TURNS:        <one line>
MODE:                <uncanny / eerie / weird / fantastic hesitation / fairy tale>
READINGS HELD OPEN:  <the rational reading; the supernatural reading; what keeps both alive>

STAYS DARK:          <the nature of the strange; the machinery>
STAYS CLEAR:         <what the people do and suffer, and why; the cause-and-effect chain>

PLACE:               <named exactly; what in it is humdrum; what is already slightly wrong>
POINT OF VIEW:       <who; reliable or not; whose beliefs the story needs (the skeptic?)>
TENSE:               <present for immediacy / past for inevitability / any flash-forward>
TITLE:               <the assumption it plants; how the ending overturns it>

NORMAL BASELINE:     <how the lead reacts to ordinary things, so a non-reaction can signal>
FIRST ODD DETAIL:    <small, specific, with an outsized reaction>
ESCALATION:          <each step building on the last>
THROWAWAY TURN:      <the moment past which no rational account survives; shown without comment>
GHOST / DOUBLE / DOLL: <if any: purpose, rules, why now; or which of Freud's two kinds>
COMIC REGISTER:      <none / deadpan / dry / rug-pull>

ENDING:              <human outcome closed; the strange left open; loss, not a bow; a circle?>
PROSE NOTES:         <the slightly wrong details; fresh figures; what is never described>
```

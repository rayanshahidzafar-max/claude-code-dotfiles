# The Suspense Sheet

Fill this in during development and plotting, then paste it into the story folder's `02-design.md` under a `SUSPENSE SHEET` heading. `story-design` reads it before building the step-outline, and `reader-brain` checks it at its station.

```
SUSPENSE SHEET — <working title>

GERM:            <one line>
SECOND GERM:     <if the first needed one; usually a stronger motive>
EFFECT:          <tragic / comic / both / cruel-fate reporting>
FATE OF THE LEAD: <victor or vanquished; caught, free, dead>

MODE:            <suspense / dramatic irony / mystery (closed or open)>
DANGER LINE:     <the violence or death that stays close all the time, and who it threatens>
WHO KNOWS WHAT:  <the real timeline; what each major character believes; what the reader believes, by act>

THE CRIMINAL:    <who; pleasant qualities or the dark-fascinating route>
THE COUNTERPART: <the decent one, or the lesser character readers can stand beside>
BINDING FORCES:  <what ties the two leads together: knowledge, debts, resemblance, love-hate>
POINT OF VIEW:   <single (intensity) or two contrasting (pace); first person only by choice>

OPENING:         <first sentence that moves; the situation before the reasons>
LINES OF ACTION: <ch1: wishes, inabilities, wants, dangers>

THICKENERS:      1. <witness / means>  2. <vulnerability that turns good into suspicion>
                 3. <rival who profits> 4. <the lead's own record turned against him>
COINCIDENCES:    <each one, where it falls, why it's plausible; none in the ending>
CLIMAX:          <where it falls (mid / three-quarters / end) and whether it ends the book>
THE LOCK:        <how the ending holds: mutual alibi, blackmail resolved, the truth unprovable>

CHAPTER POINTS:
| Ch | Advances the story how? | Points (1–3) | Emotional bang |
|---|---|---|---|

CLUES (mystery):
| Clue | Planted (ch) | Seen by | Pays off (ch) | False lead? Explained where? |
|---|---|---|---|---|

PLAUSIBILITY LOG: see references/plausibility.md
```

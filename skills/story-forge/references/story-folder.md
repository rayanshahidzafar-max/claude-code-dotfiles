# The Story Folder

Every story gets its own folder, and every station leaves a numbered file. The folder is the record of how the story was made. Anyone, including a future session, can see what was decided and why, and re-run any station.

```
stories/NNN-slug/
  00-brief.md          station 0
  01-world.md          station 1 — worldbuilding dossier (only what this story needs)
  02-design.md         station 2 — design sheet + step-outline (story-design)
  03-reader-check.md   station 3 — reader report on the design (reader-brain)
  04-draft-1.md        station 4 — door-closed draft, untouched after it's finished
  05-reread-notes.md   station 6 — the cold read: holes, motivation, recurring elements, theme
  06-draft-2.md        station 7 — second draft (first minus ~10%)
  07-revision-log.md   stations 7–9 — every pass: what changed and why, with word counts
  08-final.md          station 9 — the finished story
```

## 00-brief.md

```
FORM / TARGET LENGTH:
GENRE (one you love to read):
IDEAL READER (a specific person, and what delights or bores them):
THE SITUATION (what if…?):
WHY THIS STORY NOW (what the writer can't stop thinking about):
CONSTRAINTS (world, canon, names to avoid, rating):
```

- The Ideal Reader is one person, named in the brief. Every later judgment about pace or clarity is made by imagining their reaction (see `door-open.md`).
- The situation is the seed. Write it as a what-if, not a plot summary.

## 04-draft-1.md

- Leave it exactly as written once it's finished. The revision log measures the second draft against it.
- Put the word count at the top.

## 07-revision-log.md

```
DRAFT 1 WORDS:          ####
DRAFT 2 WORDS:          ####   (target: draft 1 − 10%)   actual cut: ##%

PASS  | station / skill      | what I looked for        | what changed (examples)             | words ±
1     | reread (King)        | holes, motivation, theme | …                                   |
2     | structure (McPhee)   | lead, order, high moment | …                                   |
3     | greening (McPhee)    | lines nobody will miss   | …                                   |
4     | sentences (Klinkenborg) | what each sentence says | …                                 |
5     | audit (Strunk & White)  | rules, reminders, usage | …                                  |
```

- Log examples with before and after quoted, so the reasoning can be checked later. Record the numbers: the ten-percent cut is a formula, so measure it.

## 08-final.md

- Real title, final text, word count. Note the controlling idea in one line at the bottom of the log, never inside the story.

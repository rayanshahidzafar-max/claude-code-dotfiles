# Captions, Emails, Web, CTAs, Bullets and Objections

## Captions and social posts

- Text too long kills a social post; trim to what the image can't say. [Vaynerchuk R3]
- One call to action per post. [Vaynerchuk R3]
- Don't answer your own question in the caption; leave it for the audience. [Vaynerchuk R3]
- Keep the slang and voice through to the ask, so the call to action sounds like the rest of the post. [Vaynerchuk R4]
- Link straight to the exact page promised, never the homepage. [Vaynerchuk R3]
- A right hook works when the call to action is simple, built for mobile and true to the platform. [Vaynerchuk R1]
- Captions drive engagement on image platforms. [Kane ch8]
- Subtitle every video. [Kane ch5]
- The first sentence of a caption should be short enough to read at a glance. [Sugarman ch5]
- A caption under a photo is read more than body copy; use it to pull people into the copy. [Sugarman ch4]

## Email

- An email subject line is the envelope: it must get the email opened. [Sugarman ch36]
- Keep emails brief and personal, and link to the longer piece. [Sugarman ch36]
- A teaser email's only job is the click; don't sell in it. [Edwards S18]
- Teaser shape: greeting, a surprising line or question, two to four curiosity lines, the call to action, a personal sign-off. [Edwards S18]
- A catalog cover works like a headline; a letter from the founder works like a personal email. [Sugarman ch36]

## Web pages

- Web readers leave faster than print readers, so the slide must start at once. [Sugarman ch36]
- Put buy buttons throughout a long page, not just at the end. [Sugarman ch36]
- Make the offer simple and ship fast. [Sugarman ch36]
- Stack bonuses with partners on launch day. [Sugarman ch36]
- Blog posts can ride current events and names. [Sugarman ch36]

## Calls to action

- Ask for the order clearly. [Sugarman ch18]
- Make ordering easy. [Sugarman ch18]
- Give value first, then pivot with a "by the way" to the offer. [Edwards S25]
- An ad should pull in the right people and let everyone else scroll past. [Edwards S28]
- Test many versions of an ad; ads wear out. [Edwards S28]

## Bullets

- Bullets build curiosity and carry information fast. [Edwards S9]
- Formula: it does this, so you can do that, which means this for your life. [Edwards S9]
- Five strong bullets beat thirty good ones; write a dozen and keep the best. [Edwards S9]

## Objections

- Anticipate each objection and answer it in the copy before the reader raises it. [Sugarman ch18]
- Raise a flaw yourself and resolve it; it builds credibility. [Sugarman ch19]
- Explain why a low price is low. [Sugarman ch19]
- Ask converted customers what nearly stopped them; that's the objection to answer. [Ellis ch6]

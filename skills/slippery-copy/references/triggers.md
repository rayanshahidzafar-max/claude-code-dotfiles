# The Psychological Triggers

Pick the few that fit the product and the prospect. Never fake one.

- Involvement and ownership: get the reader imagining holding and using it. [Sugarman ch19]
- An involvement device, like finding a deliberate mistake for a discount, gets people acting. [Sugarman ch19]
- Honesty: admit a flaw, then resolve it. Consumers are smart. [Sugarman ch19]
- Integrity: a clean layout and straight words. [Sugarman ch19]
- Credibility: explain a low price, raise and resolve objections, use a credible venue and name. [Sugarman ch19]
- Value and proof: compare, and educate people about why it's worth it. [Sugarman ch19]
- Justify the purchase: give logical reasons to back the emotional choice. [Sugarman ch19]
- Greed: a genuine bargain, or a price correction. [Sugarman ch19]
- Authority: a true superlative claim. [Sugarman ch19]
- Reassurance: people want confirmation after they buy. [Sugarman ch19]
- Satisfaction conviction: go beyond the usual guarantee; it can double response. [Sugarman ch19]
- Nature of the product: sell it the way its kind is bought. [Sugarman ch19]
- Nature of the prospect: speak to their real motives. [Sugarman ch19]
- Current fads: enter early, and expect the link to fade. [Sugarman ch19]
- Timing: test when it runs. [Sugarman ch19]
- Linking: tie the new to the familiar. [Sugarman ch19]
- Consistency: make the first commitment easy, then offer the add-on. [Sugarman ch19]
- Harmonize with the market: sell what people want now before your passion project. [Sugarman ch19]
- Desire to belong: the people who buy this are people like you. [Sugarman ch19]
- Desire to collect: send a holder with the first piece. [Sugarman ch19]
- Curiosity: don't show everything; let them want to see. [Sugarman ch19]
- Urgency: must be true, such as a real limit or price rise. [Sugarman ch19]
- Fear: use carefully. [Sugarman ch19]
- Instant gratification: ship fast. [Sugarman ch19]
- Exclusivity and rarity. [Sugarman ch19]
- Simplicity: one product beat nine; a simple offer beat a complex one. [Sugarman ch19]
- Human relationships: a hand holding the product, humour, a byline. [Sugarman ch19]
- Storytelling: catch attention, connect to the product, bond. [Sugarman ch19]
- Mental engagement: let the reader draw the conclusion. [Sugarman ch19]
- Guilt: give so much they feel they should respond. [Sugarman ch19]
- Specificity: exact numbers are believed. [Sugarman ch19]
- Familiarity: familiar words and repetition build comfort; don't kill a campaign because you're bored of it. [Sugarman ch19]
- Hope: never promise specific results you can't deliver. [Sugarman ch19]
- Cialdini's six (reciprocity, commitment, social proof, authority, liking, scarcity) apply to every page, and scarcity must be genuine. [Ellis ch8]
- Specific, credible testimonials with a real photo beat vague praise. [Ellis ch8]

# What the Rest of the Room Adds

## Making jabs land

- Tailor each piece for the platform: length, title, starting point. [Kane ch4]
- Cut one long piece into many short clips, each in a few formats, and test them. [Kane ch1]
- Ask whether the piece will make someone laugh, cry, smile or feel strongly; if not, it won't travel. [Kane ch3]
- Relatable, vulnerable personal truth beats polish. [Kane ch5]
- Adjacent lifestyle moments can outperform the product itself. [Kane ch5]
- Each post must stand alone. [Kane ch5]
- Content that's useful to one friend is forwarded. [Berger ch5]
- A high-arousal emotion moves people to share. [Berger ch3]
- A brand that's the point of the story stays in the retelling. [Berger ch6]
- Visible, public use spreads by imitation; put the logo where onlookers see it. [Berger ch4]
- Make the sharer look good. [Berger ch1]
- Start with a short, offbeat story tied to the product. [Sugarman ch8]
- Write one person to one person. [Sugarman ch15]
- Feeling drives the purchase; reasons defend it later. [Sugarman ch11]

## Making the ask land

- The first sentence of a caption should be short and pull the second. [Sugarman ch5]
- Curiosity drives clicks; one clear call to action takes them. [Edwards S28]
- The ask should send people to a page that keeps the promise of the post. [Ellis ch6]
- Make the first step after the click short and clear: relevance, value, one call to action. [Ellis ch6]
- Ask for small commitments first; bigger ones follow. [Eyal ch5]
- One clear next step removes hesitation. [Eyal ch2]

## Keeping the audience

- Posting at a predictable rhythm teaches the audience when to look. [Eyal ch8]
- Owned channels such as a newsletter keep the relationship when platforms change their rules. [Schaefer ch7]
- Over-reliance on one platform is a common cause of stalled growth; rules change. [Ellis ch9]
- Social sharing outside the community is the key metric after sales. [Schaefer ch10]
- Content that is relevant, interesting, timely, entertaining and superior earns people's time. [Schaefer ch10]
- Go where the audience already gathers instead of building your own house first. [Schaefer ch9]
- Measure shares, not views. [Kane ch5]

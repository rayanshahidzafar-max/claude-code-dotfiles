# Micro-content, Trends and Community Management

## Micro-content plus community management

- Micro-content plus community management is what makes social work. [Vaynerchuk R2]
- Talk about what the audience is talking about; when they switch, switch. Repeat. [Vaynerchuk R2]
- A campaign isn't six months; it's 365 days with new content daily. [Vaynerchuk R1]
- Run several ideas on different platforms and take the strongest as the seed for big spend. [Vaynerchuk R1]
- The story can change daily as long as the brand stays true. [Vaynerchuk R1]
- Data tells you when the audience wants to hear from you. [Vaynerchuk R1]
- Boxers study film; study the analytics. [Vaynerchuk R1]
- Add a social layer to every channel, even old media. [Vaynerchuk R1]

## Effort

- After the content and its setting comes effort, which lets a small brand match a bigger budget. [Vaynerchuk R9]
- Reply to comments, tag people, clear up confusion, be charming. [Vaynerchuk R9]
- The quality of the conversation beats its volume. [Vaynerchuk R9]
- Never rest on a win. [Vaynerchuk R9]

## Trendjacking

- Ride a trending moment by finding an unexpected but honest connection to the brand. [Vaynerchuk R4]
- Be ready: a team that can move in minutes, on-brand, with no hard sell. [Vaynerchuk R2]
- A trending topic plus a brand twist makes a shareable piece. [Kane ch5]
- Enter a trend early; the link fades when the trend does. [Sugarman ch19]
- A frequent everyday cue keeps a brand talked about longer than a one-off trend. [Berger ch2]

## Own the media

- Every company is now a media company; own your media. [Vaynerchuk R10]
- A tyre company's restaurant guide and a brewery's book of records show brands publishing for decades. [Vaynerchuk R10]
- Long-form lives alongside micro-content. [Vaynerchuk R10]
- Few marketers actually learn the tools; the ones who do win. [Vaynerchuk R11]
- Move fast onto new platforms, test, learn and watch others. [Vaynerchuk R12]

## The rest of the room

- A follower is a weak link; a community is members connected to each other. Jabs that get followers talking to each other build the stronger bond. [Schaefer ch2]
- Facilitate (ask) rather than manage (tell). [Schaefer ch4]
- Recognise newcomers fast; status keeps people engaged. [Schaefer ch9]
- Respond quickly; treat the audience as a partner. [Kane ch4]
- Every reply is a tribe reward that brings the person back. [Eyal ch4]
- Polls and questions give people a small investment in what comes next. [Eyal ch5]

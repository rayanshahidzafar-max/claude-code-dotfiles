# Alliances, Platforms and Staying Power

## Alliances

- Go where the audience already is. [Kane ch6]
- Partner with quality accounts, not just big ones. [Kane ch6]
- Find superconnectors who know everyone. [Kane ch6]
- Work out what's valuable to the partner before you ask. [Kane ch6]
- Trade shares for shares. [Kane ch6]
- Start with partners you can actually get, then climb. [Kane ch6]
- Engagement groups and headline-grabbing partnerships can kick-start reach. [Kane ch6]
- License content to and from others. [Kane ch6]
- Join existing communities as a helpful equal rather than building your own house first. [Schaefer ch9]
- Pick channels by cost, targeting, control, time to set up, time to results and scale, and focus on one or two. [Ellis ch5]

## Global reach

- Reach is cheaper in emerging markets; seed engagement there cheaply, then show the piece to the core audience. [Kane ch7]
- Content that isn't tied to one language travels. [Kane ch7]

## Per platform

- Instagram grows more slowly; use networks and shoutouts, get big accounts engaging, write captions that drive comments, show behind the scenes, and start with smaller influencers. [Kane ch8]
- YouTube rewards watch time. Grow through search and collaborations, post consistently, and measure follow-on views. [Kane ch9]
- On YouTube keep a strong point of view on one topic, make it the same but different, feature fans, and give cues to keep watching. [Kane ch9]
- On intimate, disappearing-video platforms, be interactive, feature fans, be real, allow mistakes, post daily, and use shoutouts and collaborations. [Kane ch10]
- On LinkedIn, reach out with value first: a specific compliment, a real achievement, an offer that makes them look good to their boss. [Kane ch11]
- Everyone hates being sold and loves to buy. [Kane ch11]
- On LinkedIn, share simple updates weekly, personal is fine, ask questions that invite comments; engagement spreads to the network's network. [Kane ch11]
- Quality connections beat count; aim like a sniper, not a shotgun. [Kane ch11]
- Refresh images; people stop seeing an image after two or three sightings. [Kane ch11]
- Tailor each piece per platform: length, title, start point. [Kane ch4]
- Break each platform into building blocks (text, image, tone, links) and tell the same story natively on each. [Vaynerchuk R11]
- Be first on new platforms and test. [Vaynerchuk R8]

## Staying power

- Lead with who you are, not just what you do. [Kane ch12]
- Earn trust and treat fans as friends. [Kane ch12]
- Give the best user experience you can. [Kane ch12]
- Adapt, be patient, and don't quit. [Kane ch12]
- Habits keep followers coming back without being chased. [Eyal, Intro]

# Targeting

## Narrow first

- Target specifically. A niche audience responds; a general one scrolls past. [Kane ch2]
- Checklist: gender, age, the goal or action you want, location. [Kane ch2]
- Interests: music, sports, brands, stores, daily routine, events, values, hobbies, shows, celebrities. [Kane ch2]
- Lifestyle, and fans of competitors. [Kane ch2]
- Test combinations separately so you know which slice responded. [Kane ch2]
- Talk to the target audience offline as well. [Kane ch2]
- Mine analytics, past orders and surveys for who actually buys and shares. [Kane ch2]
- Testing taglines on huge numbers of people in two days is now cheap; use it the way films use test screenings. [Kane ch2]
- Don't assume you know your audience. [Kane ch2]
- What people do right after viewing (sharing, clicking) shows who the real audience is. [Kane ch2]
- Choose the platform's objective carefully; it decides who is shown the content. [Kane ch2]
- The further an audience is from your core, the less it converts. [Kane ch2]
- Target shared interests and values, not just demographics. [Schaefer ch7]
- Find the people who'd be very disappointed to lose the product; they show you who to target. [Ellis ch2]

## Sharers vs buyers

- Two strategies: target the exact buyer with a direct call to action, or target the people who will share. [Kane ch2]
- Sharers may not be buyers. Daughters shared a Mother's Day video with their mothers, who were the buyers. [Kane ch2]
- Decide which you're after before you write the piece. [Kane ch2]
- Narrow content reminds the reader of one friend who needs it. [Berger ch5]
- If fewer than about four in ten users would be very disappointed to lose the product, fix the product or the audience before growing. [Ellis ch2]
- Ask the people who love it what they would use instead, what the main benefit is and who it suits best; that tells you where to find more of them. [Ellis ch2]
- Find the communities where these people already gather, the way an early payment service went where online sellers already were. [Ellis ch2]
- Know the prospect's real motives, not the ones they state. [Sugarman ch19]
- What people say and what they do differ; target by what they do. [Eyal ch2]
- People share what makes them look good to the friends they send it to. [Berger ch1]
- Look for fans of a nearby interest and follow them where they lead you, even off your plan. [Schaefer ch8]

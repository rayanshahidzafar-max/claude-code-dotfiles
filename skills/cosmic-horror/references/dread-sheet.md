# The Dread Sheet

Fill this in before drafting, then paste it into the story folder's `02-design.md` under a `DREAD SHEET` heading. `story-design` reads it before the step-outline, and `reader-brain` checks it at its station.

```
DREAD SHEET — <working title>

THE VIOLATION:     <one line: what should not be, and is>
SOURCE OF DREAD:   <puppet / self / knowledge / indifferent cosmos / pain / the familiar turned wrong>
WHAT DIES:         <body / mind / identity / belief / the lead's idea of the universe>
VALUE LADDER:      <e.g. consciousness → unconsciousness → death → worse than death, act by act>

THE LEAD:          <who; what the reader must believe about them for the reduction to hurt>
DEFENSES:
  isolation:       <what they refuse to think about>     → breaks when: <scene>
  anchoring:       <faith / family / law / profession>    → breaks when: <scene>
  distraction:     <work / screens / noise>               → breaks when: <scene>
  sublimation:     <the art or theory they make of it>    → breaks when: <scene>

THE PLACE:         <one unchanging locale; how its fate is bound to the people in it>
MOOD:              <the single human mood the story crystallizes>
ATMOSPHERE ANCHOR: <the death the atmosphere flows back from, set from page one>

GLIMPSED:          <what the reader sees of the thing; in what fragments, in what order>
NEVER NAMED:       <what stays dark, including the machinery>
SKEPTIC:           <who disbelieves, and how the reader travels with them>
MECHANICS SHOWN:   <how the characters got from here to there: must be clear>

ESCALATION:        <the slightly wrong details, in order; the point past which nothing can be explained away>
ENDING:            <no back door; the world goes on; what the lead can no longer do>
PROSE NOTES:       <the concrete figures; banned adjectives; where language is allowed to fail>
```

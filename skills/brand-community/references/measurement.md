# Measurement

- Direct marketing chases a coupon redemption; brand marketing builds a feeling, like a sports drink linked to a winning team's celebration. Community belongs to brand marketing. [Schaefer ch10]
- Don't force direct attribution on community. [Schaefer ch10]
- Most companies keep communities stuck on support because support is easy to measure. [Schaefer ch10]
- After sales, the most important metric is sharing outside the community. [Schaefer ch10]
- Help members spread it: early or exclusive access, experiences, samples, things to share. [Schaefer ch10]
- Hero products give members something to rally around. [Schaefer ch10]
- RITES content is relevant, interesting, timely, entertaining and superior. [Schaefer ch10]
- Engagement rate is the leading indicator. [Schaefer ch10]
- Bigger dilutes connection. [Schaefer ch10]
- The sticker test: would members put your logo on their laptop or phone? [Schaefer ch10]
- At one beauty retailer, loyalty members made up the vast majority of sales. [Schaefer ch10]
- Track engagement rate, growth (best through referrals) and mentions outside. [Schaefer ch4]
- Digital tokens can mark membership and status, but treat them with caution. [Schaefer ch11]
- AI can help community managers sort conversations. [Schaefer ch11]
- Promises built into tokens must be kept forever. [Schaefer ch11]
- Transparency and long-term commitment matter; a broken promise destroys trust. [Schaefer ch11]
- Group members by when they joined and see who stays. [Ellis ch7]
- Use shares, not likes, as the signal of value. [Kane ch5]

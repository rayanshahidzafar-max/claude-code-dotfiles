# Leadership, Status and the Social Contract

## The community manager

- The community manager sits under marketing and becomes the face and friend of the community. [Schaefer ch9]
- Members form one-sided bonds with that person; it's part of the value. [Schaefer ch9]
- Status is the fuel. Acknowledge newcomers fast and give playful levels. [Schaefer ch9]
- A community is a social contract; abandoning it breaks trust. [Schaefer ch9]
- Don't build your own house; go where they already gather. [Schaefer ch9]
- Join existing communities as a helpful equal; one executive became an admin that way. [Schaefer ch9]
- A music platform's ambassador programme granted status and perks, judged on brand value not sales, grew carefully and put member-to-member bonds first. [Schaefer ch9]

## The ladder

- Give roles: welcomers, connectors, rule-keepers, and paid roles for the most passionate. [Schaefer ch4]
- The goal is a community that sustains itself without the founder. [Schaefer ch4]
- Boundaries create safety and freedom, like a dog in a fenced park. [Schaefer ch4]
- Loyalty shows when members defend you. [Schaefer ch4]
- Promote attendees to speakers and hosts. [Schaefer ch2]
- Ambassador programmes and recognised achievements keep people. [Ellis ch7]

## Ground rules and crisis

- Set ground rules early and remove toxicity. [Schaefer, Conclusion]
- People who joined to make money nearly wrecked one community; in a crisis tell members it's bad and you're staying. [Schaefer, Conclusion]
- Be proud of the work. [Schaefer, Conclusion]
- Watch for angry words and deal with the problem before it spreads. [Berger ch3]

## Digital campfires

- Private messaging, micro-communities and shared experiences like games and live streams are where people gather now. [Schaefer ch12]
- Earn credibility with perks, co-creation, customisation, moderation and steady recognition. [Schaefer ch12]
- Give each campfire dedicated, culturally specific effort. [Schaefer ch12]
- Keep a narrow focus; the campfire complements the public billboard. [Schaefer ch12]
- Partner with existing campfires. [Schaefer ch12]
- Insider access makes people feel special, and insiders talk. [Berger ch1]

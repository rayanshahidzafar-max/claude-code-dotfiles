# Activities, Questions, Polls and Replies

## Activities

- Earn people's time: the community must beat scrolling. [Schaefer ch7]
- Activities should be purposeful, participatory and repeatable. [Schaefer ch7]
- Exceed expectations or redesign. [Schaefer ch7]
- Start before it's perfect; members will improve it. [Schaefer ch7]
- Give early courses away free, run group challenges with photo proof, keep a newsletter as a direct line. [Schaefer ch7]
- When it gets too big, deputise leaders. [Schaefer ch7]
- Record birthday and hard-day videos for members. [Schaefer ch4]
- Celebrate everyone, not just winners. [Schaefer ch4]
- A group without a shared activity bombed; an activity gave people a reason to stay. [Schaefer, Conclusion]
- Experiential marketing gives members something to do, not just read. [Schaefer ch7]

## Questions and polls

- A poll returns answers in hours; use it for decisions and for insight. [Schaefer ch3]
- Ask members to vote on products, names and designs. [Schaefer ch3]
- Ask what the community wants before deciding; facilitating means asking. [Schaefer ch4]
- Polls invite easy engagement and tell you what to post next. [Vaynerchuk R3]
- Ask the audience about their lives and interests, then make content about those. [Vaynerchuk R3]
- Ask questions that invite comment. [Kane ch11]
- Don't answer your own question. [Vaynerchuk R3]
- Ask members how disappointed they'd be if the product disappeared, to find who loves it most. [Ellis ch2]

## Replies

- Acknowledge newcomers fast. [Schaefer ch9]
- Reply to comments, tag people, clear up confusion and be charming. [Vaynerchuk R9]
- Respond quickly; the audience is a partner. [Kane ch4]
- Feature members' work and stories in your own content. [Schaefer ch8]
- A reply is a tribe reward; it brings people back. [Eyal ch4]
- A message that expects a reply loads the next visit. [Eyal ch5]
- Surprise-and-delight one member and the story travels. [Vaynerchuk R4]

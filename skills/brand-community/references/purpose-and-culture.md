# Purpose, Culture and Power

## Why community now

- Ads are fading: people block them, skip them and trust them less. [Schaefer ch3]
- Early brand communities failed on slow tech, expensive owned sites and company-centred design. [Schaefer ch3]
- A beloved neighbourhood shop is the first and only kind of marketing people actually want. [Schaefer ch3]
- Community differentiates: leaving the brand means leaving people, an emotional switching cost. [Schaefer ch3]
- It gives continuous insight and fast answers; a poll can return in hours. [Schaefer ch3]
- People trust information from a community far more than from the company. [Schaefer ch3]
- It builds advocacy, loyalty and attachment. [Schaefer ch3]
- Members co-create: voting on products, suggesting designs, naming things. [Schaefer ch3]
- Some brands sell paid access to peers as a service. [Schaefer ch3]
- Belonging boosts self-worth and makes people advocates. [Schaefer ch3]
- A sportswear brand ran in-store classes and picked local ambassadors a year before opening a store. [Schaefer ch3]

## The framework

- Six parts: culture, purpose, gathering, redistribution of power, monetisation, measurement. [Schaefer ch5]
- Small regular gatherings around topics members love beat big events where people stay anonymous. [Schaefer ch5]
- A tribe that loves you sells for you. [Schaefer ch5]

## Culture

- Community is business strategy, not only marketing. [Schaefer ch6]
- It exists to serve people, not the business; constant selling is the fatal flaw. [Schaefer ch6]
- Create leaders from within: find the catalysts and let them fly. [Schaefer ch6]
- In-person bonds deepen online ones. [Schaefer ch6]
- Share control on a spectrum: open, discerning, restricted or fan-run. [Schaefer ch6]
- Change needs support from the top; ask for a six-to-twelve-month pilot with quick-win stories. [Schaefer ch6]
- A firm's weekly live show featuring its clients brought most of its new clients at a fraction of the usual cost. [Schaefer ch6]

## Purpose

- You don't control your brand; a brand is what people tell each other. [Schaefer ch7]
- Help, help, help, not sell, sell, sell. [Schaefer ch7]
- A motorcycle brand helps people be who they want to be. [Schaefer ch7]
- Target shared interests and values, not demographics. [Schaefer ch7]
- Younger buyers want brands to take a position on issues. [Schaefer ch7]
- Purpose checks: do members gain knowledge, make a change, have a feeling, get happier, healthier or richer, share experiences, or join because they believe in what you stand for? [Schaefer ch7]
- Let the community colour outside the lines; don't police brand style there. [Schaefer ch7]
- A very specific purpose beats a broad one: mothers who won't give up time with their children to build a business. [Schaefer ch4]
- Facilitate (ask), don't manage (tell). [Schaefer ch4]
- Make the customer the hero and the brand the guide. [Edwards S4]
- A story about a cause gets shared when it makes people angry at the problem or inspired that they can fix it. [Berger ch3]

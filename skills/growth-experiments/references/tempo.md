# Tempo, ICE and the Weekly Meeting

## Tempo

- A football team that skipped huddles ran more plays and learned faster; tempo is learning. [Ellis ch4]
- Most experiments fail; volume finds the winners. [Ellis ch4]
- A small monthly improvement compounds to a large yearly one. [Ellis ch4]
- Start slow and build tempo; a one-week cycle works. [Ellis ch4]

## Analyse

- Ask what features people use, which screens, how often, from which source and device, where they exit and what breaks. [Ellis ch4]

## Ideate

- Generate many ideas without self-censorship, from the whole company. [Ellis ch4]
- Idea template: a short name, a description (who, what, where, when, why, how), a cause-and-effect hypothesis with expected lift, and metrics including downstream ones. [Ellis ch4]

## Prioritise with ICE

- Score impact, confidence and ease from 1 to 10 and average them. [Ellis ch4]
- Don't argue scores too long. [Ellis ch4]
- Doubling down on a past winner raises confidence. [Ellis ch4]
- Low scorers sometimes win. [Ellis ch4]

## Test

- Aim for high statistical confidence before calling a winner. [Ellis ch4]
- The control wins a tie. [Ellis ch4]
- Write a test summary into a shared knowledge base. [Ellis ch4]
- Share wins widely. [Ellis ch4]

## The weekly growth meeting (about an hour)

- Metrics and focus area: 15 minutes. [Ellis ch4]
- Last week's tests: 10 minutes. [Ellis ch4]
- Key lessons: 15 minutes. [Ellis ch4]
- Select this week's tests: 15 minutes. [Ellis ch4]
- Grow the idea pipeline: 5 minutes. [Ellis ch4]

## The rest of the room

- Kill a creative that doesn't land within the first hour; set thresholds before you start. [Kane ch1]
- Learn why a test won, or you aren't getting smarter. [Kane ch1]
- Launch many variations at once. [Kane ch4]
- Test fundamentally different messages, not just tweaks. [Kane ch4]
- Test small before big. [Sugarman ch10]

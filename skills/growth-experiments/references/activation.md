# Activation

## The funnel

- Most traffic never activates. [Ellis ch6]
- Map every step to the aha moment. [Ellis ch6]
- Build a funnel report of conversion and drop-off, split by channel. [Ellis ch6]
- Then survey and interview to learn why people drop. [Ellis ch6]
- Exit surveys need only one or two open questions, such as what's stopping you. [Ellis ch6]
- Ask people who did convert what nearly stopped them. [Ellis ch6]
- One sales tool ran dozens of failed tests before the wins came. [Ellis ch6]

## Friction

- Growth is desire minus friction. [Ellis ch6]
- Treat the new-user experience as a product: relevance, value and a clear call to action. [Ellis ch6]
- Single sign-on removes a step. [Ellis ch6]
- Flip the funnel: let people try before signing up. [Ellis ch6]
- Some friction is fine if it leads to value. [Ellis ch6]
- Positive friction: small commitments, a sense of flow, stored value. [Ellis ch6]
- Learn flows that personalise, like choosing interests at sign-up, speed value. [Ellis ch6]
- Short questionnaires of five or fewer choices that personalise help. [Ellis ch6]
- Gamification works only if rewards fit the core value: status, access, power, stuff. [Ellis ch6]

## Triggers

- Behaviour needs motivation, ability and a trigger at the same moment. [Ellis ch6]
- Don't ask for notification opt-in too early; explain the value first. [Ellis ch6]
- Triggers should alert people to real value. [Ellis ch6]
- Keep a holdout group to measure whether triggers help. [Ellis ch6]
- Trigger types: account completion, purchase nudges, reactivation, new features, top users, status changes. [Ellis ch6]
- Internal triggers are the ultimate goal. [Ellis ch6]

## Persuasion

- Use reciprocity, commitment, social proof, authority, liking and scarcity, honestly. [Ellis ch6]
- Find the scarcest of time, money, effort, thinking, social fit or routine, and remove it. [Eyal ch3]
- Endowed progress helps people finish: start them partway. [Eyal ch3]
- One call to action per screen. [Vaynerchuk R3]
- The first sentence exists to get the second read; every screen exists to get the next one opened. [Sugarman ch5]

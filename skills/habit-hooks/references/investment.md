# Investment

## Why putting in makes people stay

- Habits form through frequency first; attitudes follow behaviour. [Eyal ch5]
- People value what they build. Effort turns into attachment, the way flat-pack furniture feels better once you assembled it. [Eyal ch5]
- People want to be consistent with what they've already done. A small public sign of support makes a bigger one far more likely later. [Eyal ch5]
- People explain their own behaviour to themselves. After repeated use they decide they like the thing, which is how acquired tastes form. [Eyal ch5]
- Investment is about the next reward, not the one just received. [Eyal ch5]
- Investment adds a little friction on purpose, because it asks for work. [Eyal ch5]

## When and what to ask

- Ask for investment after the reward, not before. People return favours, even to software that helped them. [Eyal ch5]
- Stored value makes the product better with use: content saved, data entered, followers gained, reputation earned, skill learned. [Eyal ch5]
- Stage it: small asks first, bigger ones once the habit is forming. [Eyal ch5]
- Stored value raises the cost of leaving, because leaving means losing what you've built. [Eyal ch5]
- In a community the stored value is people. Leaving means leaving friends, which is the strongest switching cost. [Schaefer ch3]
- Small commitments, a state of flow and stored value are positive friction: friction that leads to more value is fine. [Ellis ch6]
- A short questionnaire of five or fewer choices that personalises what comes next is an investment people are glad to make. [Ellis ch6]

## Loading the next trigger

- The best investments set up the next trigger: a reply someone will see, an invitation sent, a calendar link, a follow that fills the feed. [Eyal ch5]
- Messages that expect a reply bring people back when the reply arrives. [Eyal ch5]
- Tell people when someone joins their thread or acts on their work. [Eyal ch5]
- The aim is to shorten the time between one visit and the next. [Eyal ch5]
- For content, the investment might be a comment that will get a reply, a vote that decides the next topic, or a question sent in. [Eyal ch5]
- Polls and questions to the audience give them a stake in what comes next. [Vaynerchuk R3]
- Ask the audience which topics they want, then make them; the answers become tomorrow's jabs. [Vaynerchuk R3]
- Let members co-create, vote on products and name things; co-creators become owners. [Schaefer ch3]

# Variable Rewards

## Why variability works

- What drives action is the anticipation of a reward more than the reward itself. [Eyal ch4]
- When a reward is fully predictable, interest fades. Novelty keeps people coming back. [Eyal ch4]
- Rewards that come on an unpredictable schedule keep people acting more than rewards that come every time. [Eyal ch4]
- The reward must satisfy, yet leave people wanting more. [Eyal ch4]

## Three kinds of reward

- Rewards of the tribe are social: likes, comments, upvotes, being seen, feeling accepted. [Eyal ch4]
- People also learn from peers slightly ahead of them, which is why watching others succeed is rewarding. [Eyal ch4]
- Rewards of the hunt are resources and information: the next item in the feed, the next deal, the next clue. [Eyal ch4]
- A scrolling feed that cuts off the next image half-shown keeps the hunt going. [Eyal ch4]
- Rewards of the self are mastery, completion and competence: clearing the inbox, reaching the next level, finishing the set. [Eyal ch4]
- Most lasting habits combine more than one kind. [Eyal ch4]

## Fit and freedom

- The reward has to fit why people use the product. Paying people money to answer questions failed where public upvotes worked. [Eyal ch4]
- Points and badges help only when they scratch the itch the person came with. [Eyal ch4]
- Telling people they are free to say no makes them more likely to say yes. [Eyal ch4]
- Forcing a feature on users causes pushback. Leave people room to choose; obligation breeds resistance. [Eyal ch4]
- Finite variability (a series that ends, a game with a known pattern) wears out; infinite variability (other people, user-made content) keeps going. [Eyal ch4]
- Rewards that fit the core value work: status, access, power and stuff. [Ellis ch6]
- Recognise milestones and tell people when others act on their work. [Ellis ch7]

## Rewards in content

- The tribe reward for a follower is a reply. Answer comments, tag people and clear up confusion. [Vaynerchuk R9]
- Featuring fans in the content rewards them and pulls in their friends. [Kane ch9]
- The hunt reward is the useful thing or the surprise in each post. Vary format and angle within one consistent point of view. [Kane ch9]
- Make it the same but different: a known format with something new each time. [Kane ch9]
- Celebrate every member's progress, not only the winners'. [Schaefer ch4]

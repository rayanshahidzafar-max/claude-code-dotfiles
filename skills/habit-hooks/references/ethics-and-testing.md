# The Manipulation Matrix and Habit Testing

## The manipulation matrix

- Ask two questions: would I use this myself, and does it materially improve people's lives? [Eyal ch6]
- Yes and yes is a facilitator: building a habit you'd want and that helps. This is the goal. [Eyal ch6]
- Improves lives but you wouldn't use it is a peddler: often well meant, rarely works because the maker doesn't understand the user. [Eyal ch6]
- You'd use it but it doesn't improve lives is an entertainer: fine, but hits fade and need a constant fresh pipeline. [Eyal ch6]
- Neither is a dealer: exploiting people for money. Don't. [Eyal ch6]
- Even facilitators have a duty to help the few users who become unhealthily hooked. [Eyal ch6]
- Put the same check to content: would you follow this account yourself, and does a follower leave better off? [Eyal ch6]
- Community should exist to serve people, not the business; constant selling is the fatal flaw. [Schaefer ch6]
- Don't fake urgency or scarcity; it must be true. [Sugarman ch19]
- Don't nag lapsed users; learn why they left first. [Ellis ch7]

## Habit testing

- Identify the habitual users by defining a realistic frequency for how often a habitual user would come back. [Eyal ch8]
- Find the habit path: the steps the habitual users took that others didn't, such as following a minimum number of accounts. [Eyal ch8]
- Then change the onboarding so that new users take that path. [Eyal ch8]
- As a rule of thumb, if fewer than about one in twenty users are habitual, the product needs work before growth. [Eyal ch8]
- Compare groups of users who started at the same time to see whether the habit holds. [Eyal ch8]
- Look for opportunities in your own itch, in nascent behaviours spreading from a small group, in new technology, and in interface changes. [Eyal ch8]
- Group users by start date and channel; a campaign that brings in the wrong people shows up as a steep drop. [Ellis ch7]
- Find the aha moment, the point where people first get the value, and drive new users there fast. [Ellis ch2]
- For content, track who returns for the next piece and how much more they watch. [Kane ch9]

# Re-hooks and Open Loops

How to carry an audience from one post, episode or email to the next.

## The principle

- Series stories hold people by leaving a question open: conflict, mystery and then resolution, with each episode resolving one challenge and opening the next. [Eyal ch4]
- A finite story wears out once it ends; to keep people, keep opening new questions. [Eyal ch4]
- The reward must satisfy, yet leave the person wanting more. [Eyal ch4]
- The mystery of what comes next is itself a variable reward, the way people return to see the next daily verse. [Eyal ch7]
- Load the next trigger at the end of each piece, so the next one is already expected. [Eyal ch5]

## Re-hooks inside one piece

- A brief line closing a paragraph can promise more and pull the reader on: "But there's more." Use them sparingly. [Sugarman ch10]
- Promise a payoff that you reveal later in the piece. [Sugarman ch10]
- Each element exists to get the next one read, until the reader slides to the end. [Sugarman ch8]
- Anticipate the reader's next question and answer it before they leave. [Sugarman ch16]
- Give cues that keep people watching, and measure how many go on to the next piece. [Kane ch9]
- Win attention within about four seconds and skip the long intro. [Kane ch5]
- Don't give the payoff in the post if the point is the click; leave it for the other side. [Vaynerchuk R3]

## Part two

- End a part on an open question that the next part answers, after the current part has paid off its own promise. [Eyal ch4]
- Chunk long material into small daily pieces, interesting parts first, so the habit forms before the hard parts. [Eyal ch7]
- Give people a streak or checkmarks for following along; a broken streak makes them come back to complete it. [Eyal ch7]
- If someone misses a day, offer an easier path back instead of a reset that makes them quit. [Eyal ch7]
- Each piece should still stand on its own; someone arriving at part two must get value. [Kane ch5]
- A hook that doesn't match the content loses trust; the open loop must be honest. [Kane ch1]
- Tease what's coming soon to bring people back. [Ellis ch7]
- Run a series rhythm, like a weekly show featuring members; people return to see who is on. [Schaefer ch6]

## Formats

- "Part one of three" in the first second sets the expectation of return.
- End card: one line on what part two answers, and when it lands.
- Pinned comment or reply that links the series in order.
- Email: the last line opens the next email's question; the subject line answers that promise.

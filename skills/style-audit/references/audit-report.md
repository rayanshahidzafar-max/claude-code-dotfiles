# Audit Report Template

```
STYLE AUDIT — <title / file>, <date>
Words before: ####   Words after: ####

FINDINGS
#  | location   | rule   | original                         | fix                         | why (one line)
1  | ¶3 s2      | R17    | "the reason why is that"         | "because"                   | padding
2  | ¶5 s1      | R11    | "Rounding the corner, the tower…"| "As we rounded the corner…" | dangling participle
3  | ¶8 dialog  | V11    | "she said bitterly"              | "she said"                  | the line already carries it
4  | ¶9         | King   | "he hissed"                      | "he said"                   | said-bookism
...

KEPT ON PURPOSE (rule broken deliberately, passes the ear test)
- ¶12 fragment "Not again." — rhythm; the character's clipped voice.

COUNTS
Usage (R1–R11): ##   Composition (R12–R22): ##   Reminders: ##   Misused words: ##   Other flags: ##

CORRECTED TEXT
<full text>
```

Rules for the report:

- Quote the original exactly, so the writer can find it. Keep the reason to one line.
- Record deliberate exceptions in their own section. Don't "fix" them silently.
- Never rewrite the voice. The audit corrects and clarifies; it doesn't replace the writer's style. Style takes its final shape from the writer's own mind. [S&W V]
- If the same flag appears more than five times, report the pattern once with a count and three examples, and fix all instances in the corrected text.
